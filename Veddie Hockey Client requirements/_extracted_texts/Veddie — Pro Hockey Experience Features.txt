Veddie — Pro Hockey Experience Features

Make players feel like they’re arriving for a pro game from the moment they walk into the arena.

Dressing room ready on arrival: Team name on the door, clean room, lineup on the whiteboard.

Player stall nameplates: Names, numbers and team branding above each spot.

Tape and essentials station: Stick tape, sock tape, scissors, spare laces and anti-fog spray.

Fresh towel service: A clean towel for every player, with laundry handled.

Stocked hydration station: Water, electrolyte drinks and bottles ready on the bench.

Pregame and intermission music: Team-selected playlists and a dressing-room speaker.

Athletic therapist: Pregame taping, injury assessment and support during games.

Equipment attendant: Help with minor repairs, helmet screws and last-minute gear issues.

Skate sharpening: Available at the rink or through a convenient drop-off service.

Loaner gear: Spare sticks, gloves and goalie equipment for emergencies.

Premium matching uniforms: Personalized home and away jerseys, matching socks and captain letters.

Warm-up pucks ready: A proper warm-up with a visible countdown.

Player introductions: Announcer reads the starting lineup with entrance music.

In-game arena production: Music during stoppages, goal horns and team-specific goal songs.

PA announcements: Goals, assists, penalties and milestones announced.

Quality officiating: Consistent referees, a dedicated timekeeper and clear standards.

Live scores and player stats: Goals, assists, goalie stats, standings and league leaders.

Player profiles: Headshots, bios, career stats and season history.

Game filming: Full replays available after every game.

Highlight clips: Goals, saves and big plays edited for players to share.

Game photography: Professional action shots and team photos.

Broadcast games: Commentary and livestreams for marquee matchups and playoffs.

Three stars of the game: Announced afterward, with a photo and small prize.

Postgame interviews: Quick player reactions and a branded interview backdrop.

Postgame refreshments: Fruit, protein snacks or a sponsored team meal.

Weekly media coverage: Power rankings, game recaps, player spotlights and matchup previews.

Milestone recognition: First goals, 100th games, scoring records and birthdays.

All-star experience: All-star game, skills competition and captain selections.

Playoff atmosphere: Series previews, enhanced production and championship introductions.

Championship celebration: A substantial trophy, medals or rings, team photos and a trophy presentation.

Team concierge: One contact handles scheduling, roster questions, substitutes and special requests.

Reliable, desirable ice times: Organized games that start on time and fit players’ lives.

First priorities

Room setup, tape, towels, music, an athletic therapist, personalized jerseys, accurate stats and shareable highlights. These create a noticeable experience players can feel, use and show their friends.
